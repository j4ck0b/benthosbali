Why Take Your PADI Course in Bali with Benthos?

Deciding where to take a PADI course in Bali is a critical choice for any aspiring diver. At Benthos Bali Dive Resort, we prioritize quality over quantity. Unlike large "certification factories," we keep our classes small (often 2:1 or even 1:1 ratio) to ensure you feel confident, safe, and fully supported throughout your training.

PADI Open Water Diver: The Perfect Start

The PADI Open Water course is the world's most popular scuba certification. In just 3 to 4 days, you will go from zero to a fully certified diver, capable of diving down to 18 meters anywhere in the world. Our training includes:

  • PADI eLearning: Complete your theory at home before arriving, maximizing your time in the water.
  • Confined Water Sessions: Practice essential skills in our resort pool.
  • Open Water Dives: Explore the vibrant reefs of Candidasa and Padang Bai as you complete your training dives.

Continue Your Adventure: Advanced & Specialty Courses

Already certified? Take the next step! The PADI Advanced Open Water course is the most popular follow-up, allowing you to dive deeper (30 meters) and hone your navigation skills. We also offer a wide range of specialties, including:

  • Deep Diver: Explore the walls and pinnacles of East Bali.
  • Nitrox (Enriched Air): Stay down longer and feel less tired between dives.
  • Drift Diver: Master the currents of Mimpang and Tepekong.
  • Rescue Diver: Build your confidence and learn to help others in a fun and rewarding course.

Why a PADI 5-Star Center matters?

Our 5-Star status is a guarantee of first-class service, high safety standards, and environmental responsibility. We use modern, well-maintained equipment and our instructors are among the most experienced in East Bali.
